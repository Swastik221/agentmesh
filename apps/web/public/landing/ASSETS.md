# Landing assets

The opening and chapter characters are original layered SVG illustrations in
`FlatCoder.tsx`. `ChapterScene.tsx` supplies original topic-specific SVG and HTML
objects. Their poses are driven by scroll progress. No paintings, film footage,
third-party characters, or generated raster images are loaded by the page.

`mark.svg` and `social.svg` are original AgentMesh artwork. Typography uses the
application fonts and system Georgia; icons use the existing Lucide dependency.
The supplied references informed motion and composition only.
